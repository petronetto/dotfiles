# Uncertainty protocol

The most important rule of this skill: never mark a step done or move to the next step while any uncertainty exists.

Uncertainty includes, but is not limited to:

- Unclear or implied (rather than explicit) approval.
- Failing, missing, or skipped tests.
- Unresolved review comments.
- Ambiguous requirements or scope.
- Unexpected diffs or side effects.

In every such case, stop and ask the user, in plain language, whether you are allowed to proceed. Silence or assumption is never approval.
